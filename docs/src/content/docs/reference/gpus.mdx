Pneumam detects your GPU and sets itself up for it, so there is nothing to configure.

| GPU | Support | Extra |
|---|---|---|
| RTX 5080, RTX 5090 and other RTX 50 series cards | Pneumam is fastest on these cards. | `sm120` |
| RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell | It is supported. | `sm120` |
| H100 and H200 | They are supported on Linux. | `sm90` |
| RTX 4090 and other RTX 40 series cards, L4, L40 | They are supported, FP8 included. | `sm89` |
| RTX 3090 and other RTX 30 series cards, A10, A40 | They are supported, without FP8. | `sm86` |
| A100 and older cards | They are not supported. | |

:::note
Pneumam works best on an RTX 50 series card.
:::

## Check your setup

`scripts/profile_gpu.py` checks that Pneumam gives correct results on your GPU and then measures its speed.

<Tabs syncKey="os">
<TabItem label="Linux">

```bash
python scripts/profile_gpu.py \
  --checkpoint checkpoints/anima.safetensors \
  --vae checkpoints/wan21-vae.safetensors
```

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Windows">

```powershell
python scripts\profile_gpu.py `
  --checkpoint checkpoints\anima.safetensors `
  --vae checkpoints\wan21-vae.safetensors
```

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

It measures 1024×1024 and 832×1216 by default. Use `--sizes` to choose others, `--steps` to change the step count from 20, and `--out` to choose the output folder, which defaults to `profile_out`. With the `bench` extra installed, it also plots the results.
